Frequently Asked Questions

Wholesale Supply Information

Q: What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for daily delivery?

We offer low MOQs for local Kirana shops (starting from just 15-20 bread packets or buns daily). For universities, canteens, and supermarkets, tiered volume discounts apply based on daily requirement.

Q: How do I get a Free Sample Box for my shop or canteen?

Fill out the form above or tap "Request Free Sample Box 📦". Our logistics team will deliver a freshly baked sample box containing our best-selling breads, buns, and rusks directly to your business premises in Tirunelveli.

Q: What areas across Tirunelveli do you deliver to?

Our morning dispatch vehicles cover Palayamkottai, Vannarpettai, Town, Junction, Perumalpuram, High Ground, Maharajanagar, and surrounding college/university hubs daily before 6 AM.

Q: What is your replacement policy for unsold daily bread?

We offer a hassle-free replacement guarantee on unsold fresh bread packets for verified daily Kirana retail partners to ensure zero wastage and risk-free retail margins.

Q: Can we request custom weight, size, or packaging for canteen snack boxes?

Yes! We manufacture custom-sized veg puffs, egg puffs, tea buns, and branded party boxes tailored to your canteen or event catering specifications.

Q: How do credit terms and weekly billing work?

After an initial verification period, established repeat commercial partners are eligible for consolidated weekly or monthly invoice billing.
